# Albin, Algebraic Topology I (video lectures)

## Prerequisites

## Notes
Lecturer: Pierre Albin (UIUC), a first-year graduate course recorded
live. 27 lectures following Hatcher chapters 1–2: homotopy, π₁ and van
Kampen, covering spaces and their classification, simplicial and
singular homology, exact sequences, excision. Clear and well paced.
