# Alekseev, Abel's Theorem in Problems and Solutions

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: V. B. Alekseev, from V. I. Arnold's lectures to Moscow
schoolchildren. Proves the quintic is not solvable by radicals
topologically — via Riemann surfaces and monodromy — entirely
through a chain of problems, with hints and solutions. Group theory
and complex functions built from scratch. The Arnold style in its
purest form: geometry first, everything discovered by the reader.
