# Alon & Spencer, The Probabilistic Method

## Prerequisites
- [[combinatorics-basic]]
- [[probability-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Alon, Spencer. First and second moments, alterations, the
Lovász Local Lemma, correlation inequalities, martingales and
concentration, random graphs, discrepancy; "Probabilistic Lens"
vignettes between chapters. Erdős in spirit; exercises. Dense — not a
first combinatorics book.
