# Altman & Kleiman, A Term of Commutative Algebra

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Altman, Kleiman. The Atiyah–Macdonald syllabus in 26 short
chapters with exercises embedded in the text and complete solutions —
including solutions to all of Atiyah–Macdonald's exercises. Terse, so
not a first read, but an ideal answer key when formalizing Reid or
A–M in Lean. Free online.
