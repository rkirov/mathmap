# Arapura, Algebraic Geometry over the Complex Numbers

## Prerequisites
- [[smooth-manifolds]]
- [[algebraic-topology]]
- [[commutative-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Donu Arapura. Sheaves and sheaf cohomology, de Rham and
Dolbeault, Hodge theory on Kähler manifolds, then GAGA-style algebraic
methods. Treats sheaf cohomology from both the differential and the
algebraic side — exactly the bridge the differential track is for.
Read alongside Huybrechts or just before Vakil's cohomology chapters.
