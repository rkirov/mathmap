# Arnold, Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-advanced]]
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: V. I. Arnold. Newtonian, Lagrangian, and Hamiltonian
mechanics developed on manifolds, with differential forms, symplectic
geometry, and Lie groups introduced as the physics needs them.
Geometric and opinionated; the book where many first see why forms
and symplectic structures exist.
