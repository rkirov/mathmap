# Atiyah–Macdonald, Introduction to Commutative Algebra

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Atiyah, Macdonald. Short and dense; the famous exercises
carry much of the content. The canonical clean treatment, and a poor
first book for this reader. Skim it after Reid or Eisenbud, when the
ideas are already familiar and the compactness becomes a pleasure.
