# Avigad, de Moura, Kong, Ullrich — Theorem Proving in Lean 4

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Avigad, de Moura, Kong, Ullrich. The language guide to
dependent type theory as Lean implements it: universes, inductive
types, tactics, type classes, axioms. Expository, few exercises. Dip
in when an elaboration error makes no sense; connects Nederpelt–Geuvers
to what Lean actually does. Free online.
