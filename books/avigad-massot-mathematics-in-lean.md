# Avigad & Massot, Mathematics in Lean

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Avigad, Massot. Lean 4 and Mathlib taught through the
mathematics: logic, sets and functions, elementary number theory,
structures and hierarchies, groups and rings, linear algebra,
topology, calculus. A tutorial with exercise files filled in inside
the editor. The canonical on-ramp to the Mathlib idioms every other
book's exercises need. Free online, kept current.
