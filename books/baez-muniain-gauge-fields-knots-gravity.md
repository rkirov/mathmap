# Baez & Muniain, Gauge Fields, Knots and Gravity

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-advanced]]
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: John Baez, Javier Muniain. Manifolds, forms, and de Rham
cohomology via Maxwell's equations; Lie groups, bundles, connections,
curvature via Yang–Mills and Chern–Simons; then general relativity.
Extremely motivated and conversational, exercises woven into the
text. The lightest route to *why* connections and curvature matter.
