# Bonfert-Taylor, Introduction to Complex Analysis (video lectures)

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Lecturer: Petra Bonfert-Taylor (Wesleyan, Coursera). 36 videos:
complex-plane geometry, Julia and Mandelbrot sets, conformal maps,
Cauchy theory, Laurent series, residues, a glimpse of zeta. Gentle and
geometric; a first pass with Needham before Stein–Shakarchi.
