## Notes

with two cubic curves and Bézout–Pappus–Pascal before the general
theory of varieties. Examples first, historically motivated. Pairs
with Reid or Shafarevich. An extra-topics playlist covers Riemann–Roch
for curves: PL8yHsr3EFj53Rwr6ly1oUasJXR2Qerwgj.
