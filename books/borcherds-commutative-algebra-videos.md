# Borcherds, Commutative Algebra (video lectures)

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Lecturer: Richard Borcherds. 76 short videos on graduate commutative
algebra — rings, ideals, modules, syzygies, Noetherian rings,
Nullstellensatz, localization, primary decomposition, dimension.
Informal, example-heavy, always explaining why a definition exists.
Watch alongside Reid or Eisenbud; supplies the motivation
Atiyah–Macdonald leaves out.
