# Borcherds, Lie Groups (video lectures)

## Prerequisites
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Lecturer: Richard Borcherds. 12 videos: a short, example-driven tour
of Lie groups and Lie algebras. Watch alongside Stillwell's *Naive Lie
Theory*. The same channel also has short courses on representation
theory, homological algebra, number theory, and category theory.
