# Borevich & Shafarevich, Number Theory

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]
- [[number-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Borevich, Shafarevich. Algebraic number theory driven by
Diophantine problems: p-adic numbers, representations by quadratic
forms, the theory of divisibility, local methods, analytic class
number formulas, Fermat's Last Theorem for regular primes. Concrete
and problem-motivated, with exercises; a very different organization
from Marcus or Neukirch.
