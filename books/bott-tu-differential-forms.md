# Bott & Tu, Differential Forms in Algebraic Topology

## Prerequisites
- [[smooth-manifolds]]

## Notes
Authors: Bott, Tu. De Rham cohomology, Mayer–Vietoris, Poincaré
duality, Thom isomorphism, Čech–de Rham complex, spectral sequences,
characteristic classes. Topology done with differential forms, and
the clearest bridge from the differential side to the sheaf and Čech
cohomology used in algebraic geometry. Beautifully written.
