# Bressoud, A Radical Approach to Real Analysis

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: Bressoud. Analysis told through its history: the crisis
Fourier series caused in the 1800s motivates every definition
(convergence, continuity, uniform convergence, the integral).
Narrative in the extreme; a good companion that explains *why* the
rigorous definitions in Tao look the way they do.
