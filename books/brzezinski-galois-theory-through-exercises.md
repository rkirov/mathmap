# Brzeziński, Galois Theory Through Exercises

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Juliusz Brzeziński. Classical Galois theory built through
graded, concrete exercises, each chapter opened by a short summary;
includes Galois's theorem on equations of prime degree, the quintic,
resolvents, and the inverse Galois problem. Designed for self-study.
The Galois-theory sibling of Murty–Esmonde.
