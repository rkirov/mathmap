# Buzzard, Formalising Mathematics (course notes)

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: Kevin Buzzard (Imperial College). A university course in
formalizing undergraduate mathematics in Lean 4 — logic, sets,
functions, groups, filters and topology — with an exercise repository.
Pitched at mathematicians, not programmers. Free.
