# Buzzard, Automorphic Forms and the Langlands Program (video lectures, MSRI)

## Prerequisites
- [[class-field-theory]]
- [[modular-forms]]

## Notes
Lecturer: Kevin Buzzard (MSRI Summer Graduate School, 2017). 20
lectures explaining local and global Langlands by working examples —
Dirichlet characters and class field theory, modular forms,
automorphic representations of GL(n). Notes at
https://www.ma.imperial.ac.uk/~buzzard/MSRI/. The destination preview
after class field theory and Diamond–Shurman. More series indexed at
https://langlandslearninggroup.github.io/video_lecture.html.
