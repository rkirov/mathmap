# Cassels & Fröhlich, Algebraic Number Theory

## Prerequisites
- [[algebraic-number-theory]]

## Notes
Editors: Cassels, Fröhlich. The 1965 Brighton proceedings: Serre on
local CFT, Tate on global CFT, Tate's thesis, and the famous "Tate's
exercises" (Hilbert symbols, local–global, Kronecker–Weber). Terse
survey chapters — a poor first book. Buzzard hosts full solutions to
the exercises, which make a natural Lean project. Tate's thesis is the
first step toward automorphic forms.
Exercise solutions (Ni, hosted by Buzzard):
https://www.ma.imperial.ac.uk/~buzzard/Cassels_Froehlich_Exercises.pdf
