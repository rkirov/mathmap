# Chen, An Infinitely Large Napkin

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: Evan Chen. A panoramic tour from algebra and topology through
representation theory, complex analysis, measure theory, differential
geometry, algebraic topology, category theory, algebraic number theory
(through Artin reciprocity), and schemes. "Why before how", in an
olympiad teacher's voice, with problems. Read the relevant part before
starting each new course.
