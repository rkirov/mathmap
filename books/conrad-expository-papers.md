# Conrad, Expository Papers ("blurbs")

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Keith Conrad. Hundreds of short self-contained handouts on
group theory, rings and fields, Galois theory, multilinear algebra,
and algebraic number theory — each example-heavy and easy to turn
into Lean exercises. The best free second explanation for any sticking
point on the arithmetic arc.
