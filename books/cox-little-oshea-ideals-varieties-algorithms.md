# Cox, Little, O'Shea — Ideals, Varieties, and Algorithms

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Cox, Little, O'Shea. Affine and projective varieties,
Gröbner bases, elimination, the Nullstellensatz, dimension —
everything made computable. Undergraduate, exceptionally motivated,
huge exercise sets. The natural first algebraic geometry book, and it
makes commutative algebra feel necessary before you study it.
