# Crane, Discrete Differential Geometry (video lectures)

## Prerequisites
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Lecturer: Keenan Crane (CMU 15-458). About 21 lectures: curves and
surfaces, discrete differential forms and exterior calculus,
curvature, with notes and assignments on the course site. Forms and
curvature become concrete objects on meshes; a strong intuition pump
next to Hubbard, Needham, and do Carmo.
