# Diamond & Shurman, A First Course in Modular Forms

## Prerequisites
- [[complex-analysis]]
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Diamond, Shurman. Modular forms and modular curves through
Eichler–Shimura and the modularity theorem, ending with Galois
representations. Heavily exercised and motivated. Early chapters need
only complex analysis and algebra; later ones lean on
[[algebraic-number-theory]] and [[riemann-surfaces]]. The natural
book on the Langlands arc after Koblitz.
