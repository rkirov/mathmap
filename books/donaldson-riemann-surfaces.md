# Donaldson, Riemann Surfaces

## Prerequisites
- [[complex-analysis]]
- [[smooth-manifolds]]

## Notes
Author: Simon Donaldson. Uniformization and Riemann–Roch proved
analytically — solving the Laplace equation with Hilbert-space methods
and forms — plus ℘, the Abel–Jacobi map, hyperbolic geometry. Short
and motivated; shows the harmonic-theory idea behind Hodge theory in
dimension one. Complements Miranda's algebraic approach.
