## Notes
Authors: Dubrovin, Fomenko, Novikov. Geometry of surfaces,
transformation groups, and tensor fields, always tied to mechanics,
relativity, and field theory. Computational and physics-flavored,
unlike Western geometry texts; Parts II–III continue to manifolds and
topology.
