# Edwards, Fermat's Last Theorem: A Genetic Introduction to Algebraic Number Theory

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]
- [[number-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Harold Edwards. Algebraic number theory built the way it
arose: Fermat and Euler, Kummer's ideal numbers for cyclotomic
integers, FLT for regular primes, and the link to Gauss's quadratic
forms. Every concept arrives after a problem demands it; many
computational exercises. The "why" book to read before or alongside
Marcus. Same author as the historical *Galois Theory*.
