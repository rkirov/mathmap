# Erdmann & Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Erdmann, Wildon. A leisurely route to the classification of
complex semisimple Lie algebras: low-dimensional examples by hand,
sl₂ representations, root systems, Dynkin diagrams. Plenty of
exercises, solutions to those used later. Pure algebra, so the most
Lean-friendly way into Lie theory (Mathlib's Lie algebra library is
substantial). Pairs with Stillwell or Hall on the group side.
