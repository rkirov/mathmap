# Etingof et al., Introduction to Representation Theory

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Etingof, Golberg, Hensel, Liu, Schwendner, Vaintrob, Yudovina.
Algebras and modules, finite groups, symmetric groups and Schur–Weyl
duality, quivers and Gabriel's theorem, with category-theory and
homological-algebra appetizers. Much of the theory is developed in
problems (hints for the hard ones). From an MIT course; free online.
