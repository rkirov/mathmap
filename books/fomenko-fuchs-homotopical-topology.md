# Fomenko & Fuchs, Homotopical Topology

## Prerequisites
- [[point-set-topology]]
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Fomenko, Fuchs. Homotopy, fibrations, spectral sequences,
cohomology operations, K-theory, with Fomenko's surreal illustrations.
A Moscow-school graduate course; skim alongside or after Hatcher for
another route through the same ideas.
