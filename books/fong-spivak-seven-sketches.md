# Fong & Spivak, Seven Sketches in Compositionality

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Fong, Spivak. *An Invitation to Applied Category Theory.*
Posets, monoidal categories, profunctors, operads, and toposes via
applications (databases, circuits, co-design). No algebra assumed;
exercises with solutions throughout. Pairs naturally with Lawvere:
same "categories for everyone" spirit, more modern examples.
