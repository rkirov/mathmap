# Fuchs & Tabachnikov, Mathematical Omnibus

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Fuchs, Tabachnikov. *Thirty Lectures on Classic Mathematics.*
Self-contained lectures across algebra, arithmetic, geometry,
topology, dynamics — cubics, Chebyshev polynomials, the four-vertex
theorem, Poncelet, billiards — each ending with exercises. A
Russian-school "desert island book"; mostly geometric.
