# Fulton, Algebraic Curves

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]
- [[commutative-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: William Fulton. *An Introduction to Algebraic Geometry.*
Affine and projective plane curves, local rings, intersection
multiplicities, Bézout, the group law on cubics, Riemann–Roch by
Noether's elementary method. About 120 pages; the dense exercise sets
are the best part. Concrete bridge from Reid or Cox–Little–O'Shea
toward Vakil. Free PDF from the author.
