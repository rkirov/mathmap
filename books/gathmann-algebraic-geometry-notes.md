# Gathmann, Algebraic Geometry (notes)

## Prerequisites
- [[commutative-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Andreas Gathmann (2021/22, updated 2026). Affine varieties,
Zariski topology, sheaves of regular functions, morphisms, abstract
and projective varieties, dimension, smoothness. Carefully motivated
with pictures, examples, and exercises every chapter; uses sheaves
from the start so the move to schemes is smooth. Widely called the
best free first course in varieties.
