# Gathmann, Commutative Algebra (notes)

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Andreas Gathmann. The Atiyah–Macdonald syllabus — ideals,
modules, tensor products, localization, chain conditions, primary
decomposition, integral extensions, Nullstellensatz, dimension,
valuation rings, Dedekind domains — with the geometric meaning of each
notion and exercises throughout. The motivated first read, with A–M
as the later skim.
