# Gelfand, Lectures on Linear Algebra

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: I. M. Gelfand. Short lecture course from Moscow State:
n-dimensional spaces, bilinear forms, linear transformations,
canonical forms, tensors. Conversational and geometric, written as
the lectures were given. A quick read next to Axler.
