## Notes
Authors: Getz, Hahn. *With a View Toward Trace Formulae* (GTM 300,
2024). Adeles, reductive groups, unramified local representations and
the Satake isomorphism, automorphic representations, a statement of
functoriality, cohomology of locally symmetric spaces, trace formulae.
Exercises per chapter, but graduate-dense: the far end of the arc.
Read Tate's thesis and Diamond–Shurman first.
