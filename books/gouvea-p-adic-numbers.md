# Gouvêa, p-adic Numbers: An Introduction

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]
- [[analysis-foundations]]
- [[number-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Fernando Gouvêa. Absolute values, constructing ℚ_p, Hensel's
lemma, analysis in ℚ_p, finite extensions, ℂ_p. Chatty and motivated —
it explores before it proves — with about 360 exercises. Widely
recommended as the beginner's text. The motivated entry to local
fields, instead of starting from Serre.
