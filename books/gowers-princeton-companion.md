# Gowers (ed.), The Princeton Companion to Mathematics

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Editors: Gowers, Barrow-Green, Leader. A 1000-page expository survey:
concepts, branch overviews (arithmetic geometry, representation
theory, …), famous theorems and problems. Not for cover-to-cover —
read the relevant branch article before starting each new course.
