# Gross, Abstract Algebra — Harvard Math 122 (video lectures)

## Prerequisites
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Lecturer: Benedict Gross (Harvard, Fall 2003). All 38 lectures —
groups, actions, bilinear forms, rings, fields — following Artin's
*Algebra* closely, with problems assigned every lecture (syllabus:
https://abel.math.harvard.edu/archive/122_fall_03/). One of the best
recorded algebra courses: motivated, linear algebra woven into group
theory. Do it with Artin right after Axler. Note: an unofficial
re-upload; the often-cited older playlist is now incomplete.
