# Hairer & Wanner, Analysis by Its History

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Hairer, Wanner. Calculus in the order it was discovered —
Newton, Leibniz, Euler computations first (series, continued
fractions, Euler–Maclaurin), then 19th-century rigor. 173 figures;
exercises drawn from original papers. A historical skim after Tao I.
