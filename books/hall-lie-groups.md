## Notes
Author: Brian Hall. Matrix Lie groups first, so no manifold theory is
needed; then Lie algebras, the Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff formula,
sl(2) and sl(3) representations worked by hand, root systems,
highest weights. Concrete, motivated, exercised.
