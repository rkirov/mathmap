# Halmos, Finite-Dimensional Vector Spaces

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: Halmos. The 1942 classic that shaped how linear algebra is
taught (Axler descends from it). Written to generalize: operators
and duality framed as they appear in Hilbert space. Terse, elegant;
skim it next to Axler for a second perspective.
