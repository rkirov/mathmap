# Hatcher, Topology of Numbers

## Prerequisites
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Allen Hatcher (AMS 2022; free PDF). The Farey diagram,
continued fractions, linear fractional transformations, then binary
quadratic forms through Conway's topograph — Pell's equation, class
numbers, genus theory. Visual and geometric, exercises every chapter.
Leads into Cox's *Primes of the Form x²+ny²* and to SL₂(ℤ), the
doorway to modular forms.
