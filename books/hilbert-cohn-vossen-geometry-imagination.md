# Hilbert & Cohn-Vossen, Geometry and the Imagination

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Hilbert, Cohn-Vossen (1932). Curves and surfaces,
lattices, projective configurations, differential geometry,
kinematics, topology — all intuitively, with no exercises. A
visual-geometry classic from another era, in the spirit of Needham.
