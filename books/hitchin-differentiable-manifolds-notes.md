## Notes
Author: Nigel Hitchin. Manifolds, tangent and vector bundles, flows,
forms, de Rham cohomology, Stokes, a glimpse of metrics — 94 elegant
pages by a master geometer. Skim alongside Lee or Tu. His surfaces and
algebraic curves notes are on the same site.
