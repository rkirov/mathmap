# Huybrechts, Complex Geometry: An Introduction

## Prerequisites
- [[smooth-manifolds]]
- [[complex-analysis]]
- [[algebraic-topology]]

## Notes
Author: Daniel Huybrechts. Several complex variables, complex and
Kähler manifolds, Hodge theory, bundles with connections and
curvature, Chern classes, Kodaira vanishing and embedding;
appendices on sheaf cohomology. Streamlined, with illustrative
exercises. The standard modern first book — much gentler than
Griffiths–Harris, which is a poor first book.
