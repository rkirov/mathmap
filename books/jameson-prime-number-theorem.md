# Jameson, The Prime Number Theorem

## Prerequisites
- [[complex-analysis]]
- [[number-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Author: G. J. O. Jameson. Summation methods, Dirichlet series,
Möbius inversion, zeta, two complete analytic proofs of the prime
number theorem, then primes in progressions and Dirichlet
L-functions. Lucid, motivated, many examples and exercises; one big
theorem as the goal.
