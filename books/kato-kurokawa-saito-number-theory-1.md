# Kato, Kurokawa, Saito — Number Theory 1: Fermat's Dream

## Prerequisites
- [[number-theory-basic]]
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Kato, Kurokawa, Saito (Iwanami series). From Fermat's
marginal notes to modern theory: rational points on conics and
elliptic curves, p-adic numbers, quadratic reciprocity, zeta values,
algebraic number fields. Motivated, historical, example-first, with
exercises. Volume 2 continues into class field theory.
