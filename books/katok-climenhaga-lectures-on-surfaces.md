# Katok & Climenhaga, Lectures on Surfaces

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Anatole Katok, Vaughn Climenhaga. *(Almost) Everything You
Wanted to Know about Them.* Organized around the Euler characteristic,
which reappears through combinatorics, homology, Morse theory,
vector-field indices, and Gauss–Bonnet, on recurring examples. Concrete,
exercised, proves nearly everything. Example-first bridge between do
Carmo and algebraic topology.
