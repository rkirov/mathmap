# Katok, p-adic Analysis Compared with Real

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Author: Svetlana Katok. About 150 pages setting p-adic analysis side
by side with real analysis: completions, total disconnectedness,
Cantor sets, sequences and series, p-adic functions. Many exercises,
most with hints or solutions. The analysis-flavored companion to
Gouvêa's number-theoretic one; reinforces point-set topology too.
