# Khinchin, Continued Fractions

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: A. Ya. Khinchin. About 100 pages: convergents, approximation
of irrationals, and the metric theory of continued fractions
(Khinchin's constant). A model of short, lucid exposition; an evening's
read alongside elementary number theory.
