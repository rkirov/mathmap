# Kirwan, Complex Algebraic Curves

## Prerequisites
- [[complex-analysis]]
- [[algebra-basic]]
- [[point-set-topology]]

## Notes
Author: Frances Kirwan. Plane curves in ℂP², Bézout, the group law on
cubics, degree–genus via branched covers, ℘, Riemann surfaces and
differentials, Abel and Riemann–Roch. Written for undergraduates,
mixing algebra, topology, and analysis with historical motivation and
chapter exercises. An easier on-ramp than Miranda; ties to elliptic
curves.
