# Körner, Fourier Analysis

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Author: T. W. Körner. About 100 short linked essays — Fourier series,
orthogonal expansions, transforms, ODEs, Brownian motion, history,
applications. Witty and discursive, a shop window rather than a
course; a separate *Exercises for Fourier Analysis* volume supplies
problems.
