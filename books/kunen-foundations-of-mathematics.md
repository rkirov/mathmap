# Kunen, The Foundations of Mathematics

## Prerequisites
- [[set-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Kenneth Kunen. Set theory, model theory with formal proofs
and completeness, and recursion theory as one system; unusually honest
about the metatheory — what it means to formalize mathematics inside
ZFC. Compact; skim after Enderton or Avigad.
