# Laczkovich, Conjecture and Proof

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]
- [[set-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Miklós Laczkovich (Budapest Semesters). Impossibility proofs —
irrationality, constructions, transcendence of e, Hilbert's third
problem — then existence proofs: pigeonhole, Banach–Tarski, Borel
hierarchy. Deep results by elementary means, problem-driven. A short
detour with self-contained Lean-friendly theorems.
