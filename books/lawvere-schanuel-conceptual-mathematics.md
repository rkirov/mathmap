# Lawvere & Schanuel, Conceptual Mathematics

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Lawvere, Schanuel. *A First Introduction to Categories.*
Category theory from scratch with no algebra assumed: sets and maps,
composition, isomorphisms, sections and retractions, universal
properties, products and coproducts, graphs and dynamical systems as
categories, a first look at toposes. Organized as articles plus
transcribed classroom sessions — slow, conversational, concrete.
Not a substitute for [[category-theory]] (Leinster and Riehl draw
their examples from algebra), but an on-ramp that makes them read
faster.
