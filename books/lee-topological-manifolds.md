# Lee, Introduction to Topological Manifolds

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Author: Lee. Point-set topology developed with manifolds as the
running example, then fundamental group, covering spaces, and
singular homology. Same thorough, exercised style as his *Smooth
Manifolds*, which it is designed to precede. Alternative to Munkres
that doubles as a first pass at [[algebraic-topology]].
