# Liu, Algebraic Geometry and Arithmetic Curves

## Prerequisites
- [[commutative-algebra-basic]]
- [[algebraic-number-theory]]

## Notes
Author: Qing Liu. Schemes, cohomology, and curves with an arithmetic
bent throughout (schemes over Dedekind rings), then arithmetic
surfaces, intersection theory, reduction of curves, stable reduction.
Nearly 600 exercises. Not a first schemes book — the bridge from Vakil
to arithmetic geometry proper (models, reduction of elliptic curves).
