# Lovász, Combinatorial Problems and Exercises

## Prerequisites
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: László Lovász (Hungarian school). Enumeration, sieves,
permutations, graph theory, matchings, hypergraphs, Ramsey theory —
entirely as problems, each with a hint and a full solution. Designed
to be worked through as a course.
