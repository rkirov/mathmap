# Macbeth, The Mechanics of Proof

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: Heather Macbeth. An intro-to-proof course written in parallel
in English and Lean 4: calculation, logic, induction, number theory,
functions, relations, sets. Hundreds of exercises done on paper and in
Lean. Below this reader's mathematical level, but the gentlest way to
build Lean fluency. Free online.
