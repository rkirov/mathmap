# MacCluer, Elementary Functional Analysis

## Prerequisites
- [[measure-theory]]
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Barbara MacCluer. Hilbert spaces, Banach spaces and the big
three theorems, compact and self-adjoint operators, Banach and
C*-algebras, the spectral theorem, in about 200 pages. Written for
self-study, full of history and asides, good exercises. Gentler and
more motivated than Brezis or Reed–Simon.
