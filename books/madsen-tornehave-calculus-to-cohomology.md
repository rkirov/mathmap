# Madsen & Tornehave, From Calculus to Cohomology

## Prerequisites
- [[smooth-manifolds]]

## Notes
Authors: Madsen, Tornehave. *De Rham Cohomology and Characteristic
Classes.* De Rham cohomology of open sets in Rⁿ, then manifolds,
integration, Morse theory, vector-field indices, Poincaré duality,
bundles, connections and curvature, Chern and Euler classes, Thom
isomorphism, general Gauss–Bonnet. Self-contained, concrete, about
150 exercises, no algebraic topology assumed. The most on-taste road
to characteristic classes.
