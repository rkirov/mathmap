# Makarov, Goluzina, Lodkin, Podkorytov — Selected Problems in Real Analysis

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Authors: Makarov, Goluzina, Lodkin, Podkorytov (St. Petersburg).
Sequences and series, inequalities, integrals, asymptotics, convexity,
real-variable theory. Harder than Demidovich; many problems are
folklore theorems, with brief solutions. A Pólya–Szegő-level source
to dip into during advanced analysis.
