# Manin, A Course in Mathematical Logic for Mathematicians

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Yu. I. Manin. Logic written for working mathematicians:
formal languages, Gödel's theorems, computability, set theory
(including forcing), plus philosophical and historical digressions.
Opinionated and wide-ranging; skim next to Avigad or Enderton.
