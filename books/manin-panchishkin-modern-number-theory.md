# Manin & Panchishkin, Introduction to Modern Number Theory

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]
- [[number-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Manin, Panchishkin. *Fundamental Problems, Ideas and
Theories.* A survey of the whole landscape — Diophantine equations,
arithmetic of fields, zeta and L-functions, modular forms,
non-abelian class field theory and Langlands, Wiles's proof — each
theory introduced through elementary problems. No exercises: a map of
where every course on the arc is heading. Reread periodically.
