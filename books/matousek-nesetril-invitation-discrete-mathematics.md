# Matoušek & Nešetřil, Invitation to Discrete Mathematics

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Matoušek, Nešetřil (Prague). Counting, graphs, trees,
planarity, double counting, the probabilistic method, finite
projective planes, generating functions, linear algebra in
combinatorics. Selected topics in depth, over 400 exercises with
hints, built to form a problem-solving habit. A warmer primary than
West's *Combinatorial Mathematics*.
