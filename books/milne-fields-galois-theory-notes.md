## Notes
Author: J. S. Milne. Splitting fields, the fundamental theorem,
computing Galois groups, transcendence, infinite Galois theory, in
144 pages with exercises and solutions. The profinite chapter matters
later for Galois representations; a second pass after Cox or Stewart.
