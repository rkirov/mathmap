# Milne, Group Theory (notes)

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: J. S. Milne. Groups, actions, Sylow theorems, subnormal
series, representations of finite groups, with exercises and
solutions, in about 150 pages. A concise second pass for the group
theory in Pinter or Artin.
