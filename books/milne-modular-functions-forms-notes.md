# Milne, Modular Functions and Modular Forms (notes)

## Prerequisites
- [[complex-analysis]]
- [[riemann-surfaces]]
- [[algebraic-number-theory]]

## Notes
Author: J. S. Milne. Modular curves as Riemann surfaces, elliptic
functions, Hecke operators, X₀(N) over ℚ, moduli, L-series,
Eichler–Shimura, complex multiplication. More geometric than most
accounts; the arithmetic-geometric view next to Diamond–Shurman.
