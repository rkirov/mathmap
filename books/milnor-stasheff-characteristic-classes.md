# Milnor & Stasheff, Characteristic Classes

## Prerequisites
- [[smooth-manifolds]]
- [[algebraic-topology]]

## Notes
Authors: Milnor, Stasheff. Foundational treatment of vector bundles
and characteristic classes — Stiefel–Whitney, Chern, Pontryagin.
Terse but deeply influential; a jewel. Best read after Bott–Tu or Tu
has made the objects familiar.
