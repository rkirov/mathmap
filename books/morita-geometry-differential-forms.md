# Morita, Geometry of Differential Forms

## Prerequisites
- [[smooth-manifolds]]
- [[algebraic-topology]]

## Notes
Author: Shigeyuki Morita (Iwanami series). Forms, de Rham's theorem,
Frobenius, then Laplacians and harmonic forms, bundles, and
Chern–Weil theory in about 300 pages. Compact and elegant in the
Japanese textbook style. A second pass over the forms → harmonic forms
→ characteristic classes arc after Lee or Tu.
