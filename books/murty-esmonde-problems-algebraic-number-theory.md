# Murty & Esmonde, Problems in Algebraic Number Theory

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]
- [[galois-theory]]

## Notes
Authors: M. Ram Murty, Jody Esmonde. Algebraic number theory taught
through problems with complete solutions: integral bases, Dedekind
domains, ideal class groups, units, quadratic reciprocity, Dirichlet
density. Each chapter a short exposition followed by exercises that
carry the theory. A problem-first route through the course.
