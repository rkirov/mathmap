# Murty, Problems in Analytic Number Theory

## Prerequisites
- [[complex-analysis]]
- [[number-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Author: M. Ram Murty. About 500 problems with full solutions:
arithmetic functions, primes in progressions, the PNT, sieves,
Dirichlet and Hecke L-functions, p-adic methods. Works as both text
and problem book. Sibling of Murty–Esmonde; harder than Stopple.
