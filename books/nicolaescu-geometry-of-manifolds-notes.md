## Notes
Author: Liviu Nicolaescu (revised 2026, 700 pages). Calculus on
manifolds, Riemannian geometry, Chern–Weil, Hodge theory, elliptic
and Dirac operators. Many worked examples; too long to read through —
dip into chosen chapters after Lee or Tu.
