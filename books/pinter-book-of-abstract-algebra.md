# Pinter, A Book of Abstract Algebra

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: Charles Pinter. 33 short chapters, each a conversational
essay on one idea followed by a long themed exercise set that carries
the theory forward — groups, rings, fields, up to Galois theory. Most
exercises are proofs, not drill. Self-contained and cheap (Dover). The
most problem-driven first pass at abstract algebra, before or instead
of Artin.
