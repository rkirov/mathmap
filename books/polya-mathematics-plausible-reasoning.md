# Pólya, Mathematics and Plausible Reasoning

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: George Pólya. Two volumes (*Induction and Analogy*, *Patterns
of Plausible Inference*) on how mathematicians guess: generalization,
specialization, analogy, through Euler's sums and isoperimetric
problems. Exercises with solutions. The heuristic companion to
Pólya–Szegő; dip in when stuck.
