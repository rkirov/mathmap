# Pólya & Szegő, Problems and Theorems in Analysis I

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Authors: Pólya, Szegő (Hungarian school). Series, integral calculus,
inequalities, and function theory as sequences of problems in which
each builds on the last, with full solutions. Legendary; a model of
teaching theory through problems. Dip in for chapters at a time.
