# Prasolov, Elements of Combinatorial and Differential Topology

## Prerequisites
- [[point-set-topology]]

## Notes
Author: V. V. Prasolov (Independent University of Moscow). Graphs,
topology of Rⁿ (Jordan, Borsuk–Ulam), surfaces, coverings and
bundles, smooth manifolds, fundamental groups; the sequel *Elements
of Homology Theory* continues to homology and characteristic classes.
Geometric, full of striking concrete results and problems with hints
or solutions. A problem source next to Hatcher and Viro.
