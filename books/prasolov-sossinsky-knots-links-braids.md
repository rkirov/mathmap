# Prasolov & Sossinsky, Knots, Links, Braids and 3-Manifolds

## Prerequisites
- [[algebraic-topology]]

## Notes
Authors: Prasolov, Sossinsky. Knot and link invariants (Jones,
Vassiliev), braids, surface homeomorphisms, Kirby calculus, surgery,
branched coverings, skein invariants of 3-manifolds. Geometric Moscow
writing with exercises; dense in places.
