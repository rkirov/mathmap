# Richter-Gebert, Perspectives on Projective Geometry

## Prerequisites
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Jürgen Richter-Gebert. *A Guided Tour Through Real and
Complex Geometry.* The projective plane, cross-ratios, conics,
Cayley–Klein construction of Euclidean, hyperbolic, and elliptic
geometry, circles via complex numbers, incidence theorems via bracket
algebra. Leisurely, hundreds of illustrations, concrete computations.
Builds projective intuition before algebraic geometry.
