# Rudin, Principles of Mathematical Analysis

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: Rudin. "Baby Rudin": the classic terse single- and
multivariable analysis text, with hard, well-known exercises. Elegant
but unmotivated, so a poor primary text for this reader's taste.
Worth reading next to a more narrative book to see the standard
proofs stated compactly.
