# Schuller, Lectures on the Geometric Anatomy of Theoretical Physics (video lectures)

## Prerequisites
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Lecturer: Frederic Schuller (Erlangen, 2013–14). 28 lectures from
logic and sets through topology, manifolds, tensors, de Rham
cohomology, Lie groups and algebras, fibre bundles, connections.
Unusually precise yet motivated; community notes exist. The ideal
companion to Lee or Tu; later lectures preview Lie theory and
characteristic classes.
