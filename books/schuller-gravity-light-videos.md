## Notes
Lecturer: Frederic Schuller (Heraeus Winter School, 2015). 24 lectures:
manifolds, multilinear algebra, tangent spaces, connections, parallel
transport, curvature, Einstein gravity. Physics supplies the "why"
for connections and curvature — the differential track's intuition
pump. Watch alongside Lee's *Riemannian Manifolds*.
