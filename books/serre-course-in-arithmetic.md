## Notes
Author: Serre. Finite fields, p-adics, quadratic forms and
Hasse–Minkowski, Dirichlet's theorem, then modular forms for SL(2,Z)
— in about 100 pages. The model of terse elegance; everything you
already half-know from elsewhere, stated perfectly.
