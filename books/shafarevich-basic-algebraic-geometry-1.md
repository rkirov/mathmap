# Shafarevich, Basic Algebraic Geometry 1

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Shafarevich. *Varieties in Projective Space.* Classical
algebraic geometry — local structure, divisors, differential forms,
intersection numbers, curves and surfaces — built on examples, with
the algebra introduced as needed. More geometric in feel than Western
treatments. Volume 2 covers schemes.
