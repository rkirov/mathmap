# Shafarevich, Basic Notions of Algebra

## Prerequisites
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Shafarevich. A panoramic survey: what groups, rings,
fields, modules, Lie groups, and categories are *for*, with examples
from geometry, number theory, and physics, and almost no proofs.
Read it for the map of algebra, before or alongside a textbook.
