# Shen & Vereshchagin, Basic Set Theory

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Shen, Vereshchagin (Moscow State lectures). Cardinalities,
Cantor–Bernstein, the diagonal method, well-orders, transfinite
induction, ordinals, Zorn and Zermelo, Hamel bases — developed through
150+ interleaved problems in the Moscow math-circle style. Short and
fun; a better first book for this reader than Halmos, and Mathlib has
`Cardinal` and `Ordinal` to formalize against.
