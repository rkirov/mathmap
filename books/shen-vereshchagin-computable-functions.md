# Shen & Vereshchagin, Computable Functions

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Authors: Shen, Vereshchagin. Computable and enumerable sets,
universal functions, numberings, the fixed-point theorem,
reducibilities, oracles, the arithmetical hierarchy, Turing machines.
Terse but problem-driven in the Moscow style. Companion volume to
their *Basic Set Theory*; Mathlib's `Computability` gives a target.
