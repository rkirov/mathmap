# Shifrin, Differential Geometry: A First Course in Curves and Surfaces (notes)

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Theodore Shifrin. Curves and the Frenet frame, global curve
theory, fundamental forms, Gauss map, Theorema Egregium, geodesics,
Gauss–Bonnet, hyperbolic geometry, in 128 pages with 16 substantial
exercise sections. A free, more motivated counterpart to do Carmo.
