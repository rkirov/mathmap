# Shiryaev, Probability

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-advanced]]

## Notes
Author: A. N. Shiryaev, student of Kolmogorov. From elementary
probability with finite sample spaces up through Kolmogorov's
axioms, martingales, Markov chains, and stationary sequences. Rigorous
and encyclopedic but full of examples; skim for the Kolmogorov
school's view of the subject.
