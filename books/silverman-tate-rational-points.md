# Silverman & Tate, Rational Points on Elliptic Curves

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Authors: Silverman, Tate. Undergraduate elliptic curves — group law,
Mordell's theorem, points over finite fields, complex multiplication —
with only basic algebra. Example-first and exercised; the gentle
entry before Silverman's *Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves*.
