# Skopenkov, Mathematics via Problems: Part 1: Algebra

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: Arkadiy Skopenkov (MSRI Mathematical Circles Library).
Classical algebra built purely through chained problems with hints and
solutions: divisibility, arithmetic mod p, polynomials, complex
numbers, permutations, up to solvability in radicals. Math-circle
level, so it builds intuition rather than the field-extension
machinery later books assume. Short precise statements, ideal for Lean.
