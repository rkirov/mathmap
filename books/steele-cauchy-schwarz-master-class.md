# Steele, The Cauchy-Schwarz Master Class

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Author: J. Michael Steele. *An Introduction to the Art of Mathematical
Inequalities.* Cauchy–Schwarz, AM-GM, Jensen and convexity, Hölder,
Hilbert's and Hardy's inequalities, majorization. The whole book is a
guided tour of discovering proofs through challenge problems, all
solved. Short, concrete, finite — ideal Lean material.
