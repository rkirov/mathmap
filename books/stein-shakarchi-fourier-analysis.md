# Stein & Shakarchi, Fourier Analysis: An Introduction

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Authors: Stein, Shakarchi. Volume I of the Princeton Lectures. Fourier
series (convergence, summability, kernels), the Fourier transform on
R and Rᵈ with the heat and wave equations, finite Fourier analysis,
ending with a full proof of Dirichlet's theorem on primes in
progressions. Applications first; exercises plus harder problems.
Avoids Lebesgue integration, so it can follow Tao I or II.
