# Stewart & Tall, Algebraic Number Theory and Fermat's Last Theorem

## Prerequisites

## Notes
Authors: Stewart, Tall. Rings of integers, factorization of ideals,
class groups, units, Minkowski — organized around the story of
Fermat's Last Theorem, ending with an overview of Wiles's proof.
Gentler and more narrative than Marcus; exercised.
