# Stillwell, Geometry of Surfaces

## Prerequisites
- [[point-set-topology]]
- [[algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Stillwell. Euclidean, spherical, and hyperbolic planes and
their isometry groups, quotient surfaces, orbifolds, coverings, the
fundamental group, hyperbolic surfaces and Poincaré's theorem.
Historical motivation, many figures, exercises in every section. Makes
covering spaces and group actions concrete before Hatcher chapter 1.
