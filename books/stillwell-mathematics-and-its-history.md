# Stillwell, Mathematics and Its History

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Author: Stillwell. Mathematics told historically: Pythagoras, conics,
polynomial equations, complex numbers, elliptic functions, Galois
theory, non-Euclidean geometry, topology, simple groups. Most sections
end with exercise sequences that walk through real proofs. Previews
the arithmetic-geometry storyline; the purest motivated narrative.
