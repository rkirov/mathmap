# Stopple, A Primer of Analytic Number Theory

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]
- [[number-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Jeffrey Stopple. *From Pythagoras to Riemann.* Figurate
numbers, Euler's ζ(2), Bernoulli numbers, orders of arithmetic
functions, the Riemann zeta function and explicit formula, a glimpse
of L-functions and elliptic-curve L-functions. Historical, lively,
numerical experiments and exercises; careful with O-notation. Does not
assume complex analysis.
