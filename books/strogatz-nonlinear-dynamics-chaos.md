## Prerequisites

## Notes
Author: Strogatz. Flows on the line and circle, bifurcations, phase
plane, limit cycles, Lorenz, chaos, maps, fractals. Geometric and
example-first with physics and biology applications and big exercise
sets. Less rigorous than Arnold or Teschl; the intuition companion.
