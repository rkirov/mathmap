# Sutherland, Elliptic Curves (MIT 18.783 notes)

## Prerequisites
- [[galois-theory]]
- [[number-theory-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Andrew Sutherland. Group law, isogenies, endomorphism rings,
Hasse's theorem, Schoof's algorithm, ordinary vs. supersingular, then
complex multiplication, modular curves, Mordell–Weil. Concrete and
computational, with problem sets. The computing side of Silverman.
