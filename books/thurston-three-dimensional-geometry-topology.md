# Thurston, Three-Dimensional Geometry and Topology, Volume 1

## Prerequisites
- [[algebraic-topology]]
- [[smooth-manifolds]]

## Notes
Author: William Thurston, edited by Silvio Levy. Intuitive examples
of 3-manifolds, hyperbolic geometry, (G,X)-structures, orbifolds, the
eight geometries. Picture-driven, with many exercises; teaches how to
*think* geometrically, though some arguments are sketches. Skim after
Stillwell.
