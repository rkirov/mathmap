# Tokieda, Topology & Geometry (video lectures, AIMS)

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Lecturer: Tadashi Tokieda (AIMS South Africa, 2014). About 15
lectures in 35 parts: an intuition-first tour of surfaces and their
geometry with hands-on demonstrations. Exceptionally motivating, in
the Arnold spirit; watch early, before or during point-set topology.
