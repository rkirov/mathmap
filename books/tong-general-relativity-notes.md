# Tong, General Relativity (lecture notes)

## Prerequisites
- [[smooth-manifolds]]

## Notes
Author: David Tong (Cambridge Part III). Chapters on differential
geometry (forms, de Rham, Stokes) and Riemannian geometry (metrics,
Hodge star, connections, curvature), each motivated by geodesics and
physics. Famously clear; problem sheets online. The physics intuition
pump next to Lee's *Riemannian Manifolds*.
