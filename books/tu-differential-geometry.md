## Notes
Author: Tu. Sequel to *An Introduction to Manifolds*. Riemannian
manifolds and curvature, connections on vector and principal bundles,
Chern–Weil theory. Same gentle pacing and exercises as the first
volume. Covers the connections/curvature material from the
differential-geometry track in one book.
