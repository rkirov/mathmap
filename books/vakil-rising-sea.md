# Vakil, The Rising Sea: Foundations of Algebraic Geometry

## Prerequisites
- [[algebra-basic]]
- [[commutative-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: Vakil. Schemes, sheaves, cohomology — Hartshorne's material
with far more motivation, pictures, and exercises (the exercises
*are* the book). Teaches the category theory it needs in chapter 1.
Long; built for cover-to-cover reading. Free online.
