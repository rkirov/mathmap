# Vinberg, A Course in Algebra

## Prerequisites
- [[linear-algebra-basic]]

## Notes
Author: E. B. Vinberg. Groups, rings, fields, modules, linear
algebra, and a first look at Lie groups and representations, taught
with the Moscow emphasis on examples, geometry, and symmetry. Many
exercises. Could serve as a primary; listed here to skim for its
different organization next to Artin.
