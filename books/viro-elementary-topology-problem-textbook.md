# Viro et al. — Elementary Topology Problem Textbook

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Authors: Viro, Ivanov, Netsvetaev, Kharlamov (St. Petersburg). Point-set
topology, then fundamental group and covering spaces, developed as a
sequence of problems you prove yourself, with hints and solutions in
the back. Grew out of the St. Petersburg courses. A full course for
someone who learns by doing problems; free online.
