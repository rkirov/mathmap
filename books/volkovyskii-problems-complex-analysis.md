# Volkovyskii, Lunts, Aramanovich — A Collection of Problems on Complex Analysis

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Authors: Volkovyskii, Lunts, Aramanovich. Over 1,500 problems:
conformal maps, harmonic functions, series, Cauchy and residue
theorems, Laurent series, infinite products, applications to
hydrodynamics and electrostatics. Many are computational — choose
rather than work through. Problem bank next to Stein–Shakarchi.
