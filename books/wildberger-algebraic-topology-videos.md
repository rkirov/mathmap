# Wildberger, Algebraic Topology: A Beginner's Course (video lectures)

## Prerequisites
(none)

## Notes
Lecturer: N. J. Wildberger (UNSW). 40 lectures: winding numbers,
knots, classification of surfaces, Euler characteristic, links to
spherical and hyperbolic geometry, first homology. Concrete,
historical, picture-driven. Wildberger is a finitist and skips
point-set foundations; treat it as intuition, not foundations.
