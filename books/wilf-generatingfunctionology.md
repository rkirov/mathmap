# Wilf, generatingfunctionology

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Author: Wilf. Generating functions as the organizing idea of
enumeration: formal power series, the exponential formula,
asymptotics via analysis, the WZ method. Short, conversational, many
exercises with solutions. Free online. The friendly entry to
[[enumerative-combinatorics]] before Stanley.
