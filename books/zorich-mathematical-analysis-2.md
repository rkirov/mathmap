# Zorich, Mathematical Analysis II

## Prerequisites
- [[analysis-foundations]]

## Notes
Author: V. A. Zorich. Continues into multivariable integration,
differential forms on manifolds, Stokes's theorem, series and
integrals depending on a parameter, Fourier analysis, asymptotics.
More geometric and physics-aware than Western analysis texts.
