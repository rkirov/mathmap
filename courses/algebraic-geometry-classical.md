## Notes
Affine and projective varieties over an algebraically closed field,
the Nullstellensatz, morphisms, dimension, with lots of examples. Not
required for [[algebraic-geometry]] (schemes), but it supplies the
pictures that make schemes intelligible. Cox–Little–O'Shea is the
computational undergraduate entry; Harris is a tour of classical
examples.
