## Notes
Stiefel–Whitney, Chern, Pontryagin classes — topological invariants
of vector bundles, together with the forms, connections, and
curvature used to build them. Three routes: Bott–Tu through
differential forms and Čech cohomology; Tu through connections and
Chern–Weil theory; Milnor–Stasheff topologically, after
[[algebraic-topology]].
